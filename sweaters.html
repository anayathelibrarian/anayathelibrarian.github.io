Graveyard & Boneyard

Two complementary colorwork sweaters, one for me and one for my dog, Moxxi. Not a full written pattern — just the charts, for anyone who wants to work them into a pattern of their own or make it up as they go ( I did). If you're uncomfortable with three colors at a time, you can duplicate stitch third color (deaths heads, pumpkin stems, hearts on the body).

Graveyard

Mine. Workedin the round until separating under the arms, colorwork on the body and both sleeves. Grafted the shoulders together for a seamless join.

Boneyard

Moxxi's. The matching companion piece to Graveyard updated for my canine companion.

Charts are mine to share — credit appreciated, please let me know if you make something with this chart!
